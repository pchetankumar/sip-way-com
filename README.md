# SipWhey Glow Up

Build a high-converting, luxury-athletic D2C landing page and e-commerce web app for SipWhey, a premium ready-to-mix clear protein & beauty-recovery beverage brand packaged in 7-sachet weekly boxes.

Brand Identity & Aesthetic (Derived from Official Brand Logo):

Logo Element: Minimalist geometric "SW" monogram featuring angular bold black lines and metallic gold/bronze check accent encased in a gold circular ring.

Universal Color System (Flavor-Agnostic):

Primary Base: Clean pearl white and pure minimalist white backgrounds with subtle frosted-glass (glassmorphism) cards.

Brand Accents: Deep obsidian black, refined metallic gold (#D4AF37 / #C5A059), and warm champagne tones.

Flavor treatment: Keep the core website structure strictly black, gold, and white. Reserve flavor-specific colors solely for the individual product packaging mockups and discrete product pill badges so future flavor drops integrate seamlessly.

Typography: Sharp, modern, high-end sans-serif typography with high contrast and luxury spacing.

Exact Nutritional Specs per Sachet:

Total Protein: 24.5g Total Protein per Serving

20g Pure Protein from 22.5g Clear Whey Isolate for rapid muscle synthesis with zero heaviness or bloat.

5g Hydrolyzed Marine Collagen (4.5g protein) for skin elasticity, hair strength, and joint recovery.

80mg Vitamin C to optimize endogenous collagen cross-linking and antioxidant defence.

Clean Label Promise: 0g Sugar, Zero Artificial Colors, naturally flavored and crystal clear.
